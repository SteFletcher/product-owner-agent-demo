Score each PRD on every dimension from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Judge only against the intent and the PRD text; do not reward length.

- intent_fidelity: The PRD addresses what the intent asks for, no more and no less; scope and goals match.
- requirement_coverage: Requirements cover the functional, data, integration, compliance and non-functional needs the intent implies.
- persona_coverage: The people affected (customers, colleagues, partners) are identified and their changes described.
- business_outcome_coverage: Business outcomes are stated with measures that could actually be tracked.
- risk_coverage: Material risks are identified, categorised and have credible mitigations.
- constraint_compliance: Relevant policies, regulations and system constraints are acknowledged and reflected in requirements.
- internal_consistency: Scope, priorities, personas, requirements and risks agree with each other; no contradictions.
- unsupported_assumptions: Higher is better: the PRD does not invent facts, numbers or commitments beyond the intent; where it must assume, it says so.
- actionability: An engineering team could start from this PRD; requirements are testable and acceptance criteria are concrete.
- prd_completeness: All expected sections are present and substantive (overview, goals/non-goals, personas, outcomes, constraints, requirements with priorities, risks, assumptions/open questions).
