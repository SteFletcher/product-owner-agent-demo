# Intent: loyalty vouchers redeemable in store

**From:** Director of Marketing

Loyalty members earn vouchers online but can only spend them online. Store colleagues get asked about this every day and have nothing to offer. I want members to be able to show a voucher from the app at the till and have it applied, and I want the points they earn in store to show up in their wallet the same day.

Success means: in-store voucher redemptions reach 20% of all redemptions within six months, and complaints about "points missing" after a store purchase fall to near zero.

Constraints: the till software is replaced only once a year, in February, so anything needing a till change has to fit that; the same offer must behave identically online and in store because we have been caught out by that before.
