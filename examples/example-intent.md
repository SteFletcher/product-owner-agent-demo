# Intent: self-service delivery rescheduling

**From:** Head of Customer Experience
**To:** Product, Commerce Platform, Supply Chain Tech

Customers who miss a delivery, or realise they will not be home, currently have to phone the contact centre to change the delivery day. Around 9% of home-delivery orders get at least one call about delivery timing, and roughly a third of those calls are made after the order has already been dispatched. Agents can only rebook by phoning the carrier, which takes several minutes and often fails after the carrier's manifest cut-off.

I want customers to be able to reschedule a home delivery themselves from the order tracking page they already get in the dispatch email, on the website and in the app, up to the carrier's cut-off for the next day, without having to log in if they came from the email link.

What good looks like:

- A customer can pick a new delivery day (and window where the carrier supports it) in under a minute, and gets confirmation within 15 minutes.
- Delivery-timing calls to the contact centre fall by at least half within three months of launch.
- Failed deliveries fall measurably, because people change the day before the van leaves.
- Agents can see any reschedule the customer made, and can do the same thing from their desktop instead of phoning the carrier.

Things to keep in mind:

- Not every carrier can take a change in real time; for the ones that cannot, be honest with the customer about what is and is not possible rather than taking a request we cannot honour.
- We must not let someone with just a tracking link change where a parcel goes; changing the day is fine, changing the address is not part of this.
- Grocery orders with 2-hour windows are a different flow and are out of scope for now.
- This needs to land before peak; if that is not realistic, tell me what a smaller first step would be.
